Audible → Goodreads

Runs locally on this machine. Your Audible login happens in its own terminal window; your Goodreads import happens on goodreads.com in your browser — this page never sees either password.

1

Connect your Audible account

Opens a separate terminal window running audible quickstart. Log in there (2FA included) — this page has no access to what you type.

2

Export your Audible library

Pulls your full library (titles, authors, ratings, listening progress) into library.json.

3

Convert to Goodreads format

Maps each title to a Goodreads import row and looks up ISBNs via Open Library so more titles match on import. About 0.5s per title with lookups on.

4

Test import (do this first)

Goodreads imports are hard to cleanly undo. Upload a 5-title sample first and check it looks right before importing everything.

Upload test_import.csv at Goodreads → My Books → Import and export → Import books, then look at those 5 titles in your library.
